**Install or update**: one line

macOS (Apple Silicon or Intel), in Terminal:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trungkien1208/orcha/HEAD/install.sh | sh
```

Windows (x64), in PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trungkien1208/orcha/HEAD/install.ps1 | iex
```

Already on 0.1.2 or later? `orcha update` does it.

Quitting orcha leaves your agents running in the background, so the new version takes over only after a switch. To switch without stopping your agents:

```sh
orcha server live-handoff --import-exe ~/.local/bin/orcha
```

Then open orcha again.

**New in 0.11.1**
- **See the office at a glance**: once Easy Job is added, the header shows `● office` while it runs and `○ office` when it is off. Click it to open Easy Job.
- **Uninstall from COMMANDS**: every plugin has an Uninstall row. Orcha asks first, and Easy Job keeps your office data.
- **Updates reach the office every time**: an Easy Job you opened with `orcha office` now restarts on the new version after an update too, at the same address, so the page just reconnects.
- **Fixed**: an old Easy Job left behind by an earlier test or a deleted folder made `orcha office` fail with "manifest is unavailable". `orcha office install` now replaces it with the real one.
